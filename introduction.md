#Introduction

Software engineering organisations require systematic development processess to ensure that software products meet the user requirements and maintain qualities. Hence, development activities must not depend heavily on informal communication, undocumented decisions and unstructured testing because it can result in software defects.

The report examines **Online Payment with Secure2u** as a Malaysian industry reference feature and analyze its software processes, identify critical operational failures, and design a tailored transition plan to move from chaotic to disciplined process development. 

The report focuses on improving the software engineering process through five areas: formal process definition, agile requirements engineering, verification and validation, Lean and Extreme Programming practices, and process tailoring with human factors governance.

**Scope and limitation:** The report does not claim access to Maybank's confidential internal software development processes. The process audit and proposed improvements focus on our USM group's own development approach.
